### LogicFL Analysis Results

- ``applied_rules.xlsx``: applied rules to idenify fault locations.
- ``AUE.xlsx``: Average Unnecessary Examination data.
- ``exec_costs.xlsx``: execution cost information of LogicFL, AutoFL and FuseFL.
- ``exec_times.xlsx``: execution time of LogicFL.
- ``matched_results.xlsx``: matched bug results of LogicFL and the others.
- ``rank_rules.xlsx``: influence of rank candidate rules data.
- ``top10_matched_results.xlsx``: Top-10 matched results of LogicFL and the others.
- ``top10_results.xlsx``: Top-10 matched statistics for LogicFL and the others.
